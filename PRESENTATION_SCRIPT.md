Intro — ~15 seconds
Hi everyone, we are here to introduce X-Sload, an AI-powered solution for managing student stress and workload.
Have you ever thought you had enough time, but still ended up feeling overwhelmed?

Problem Statement — ~35 seconds
[CLICK — TIME ≠ CAPACITY]
University students already have calendars and to-do lists.

The real problem is that free time is not the same as available capacity.
[CLICK — WORKLOAD IS HIDDEN]
A large assignment is also more than one task. It involves research, writing, revision, and coordination. When this effort is hidden, students can easily underestimate their workload.
[CLICK — OVERLOAD COMES TOO LATE]
New commitments can also be added without seeing their impact until the workload becomes difficult to manage.
So the real problem is understanding how much workload a student can sustainably carry.

Proposed Solution — ~40 seconds
Our solution is X-Sload, an AI-powered Workload Navigator.
Instead of asking, “How much free time do I have?”
X-Sload asks, “How much workload can I sustainably handle?”
[CLICK — PERSONAL CAPACITY]
First, X-Sload considers personal capacity instead of treating every free hour as equally usable.
[CLICK — BRIEF → WORKLOAD]
Second, students can upload an assignment brief, and X-Sload breaks it into smaller tasks of approximately one hour each.
[CLICK — SHOULD I SAY YES?]
Third, students can simulate a new task or commitment and see its impact before accepting it.
[CLICK — GROUP BALANCER]
For group projects, X-Sload also supports fairer task distribution based on task complexity.
[CLICK — GUILT-FREE RECOVERY]
When plans change, the system can rebalance the workload instead of simply pushing tasks forward.
The goal is not to help students do more.
It is to help them understand what they can sustainably handle.

Workload Rebalancing — ~12 seconds
[SHOW REBALANCE]
When the workload becomes too heavy, X-Sload suggests adjustments based on available time, capacity, and deadlines.

[SHOW SPEECH-TO-TEXT]
For a daily task, students can use speech-to-text for quick input.
[SHOW IMPACT ANALYSIS]
Before adding it, X-Sload analyses its impact and highlights higher-risk situations.

Add a New Project — ~20 seconds
[SHOW DOCUMENT UPLOAD]
For larger assignments, students can upload a PDF, DOCX, or copied text.
[SHOW AI ANALYSER]
The AI identifies key information, generates a task breakdown, and supports group role assignment.

AI Task Breakdown — ~25 seconds
[SHOW TASK BREAKDOWN]
Instead of one large assignment, students receive smaller, manageable tasks that are easier to understand and schedule.
For group projects, these tasks can also be distributed according to their complexity.
This turns a complicated assignment into a clearer workload plan.

Tech Stack — ~30 seconds
To build X-Sload, we use four main technologies.
[CLICK — PRESENTATION LAYER]
Flutter provides our cross-platform mobile application.
[CLICK — BUSINESS LOGIC]
Supabase handles our backend services and business logic.
[CLICK — DATABASE]
PostgreSQL stores assignments, tasks, workload information, and group members.
[CLICK — AI ORCHESTRATION]
Finally, OpenRouter connects the application to AI models for task breakdown, workload analysis, and task distribution.
Together, these technologies provide a lightweight architecture for rapid development and AI integration.

Build Plan — ~20 seconds
Our development is divided into three weeks.
[CLICK — WEEK 1: FOUNDATION]
Week 1 focuses on assignment upload, voice input, and AI task breakdown.
[CLICK — WEEK 2: INTELLIGENCE]
Week 2 adds impact simulation, group balancing, and schedule recovery.
[CLICK — WEEK 3: INTEGRATION]
Week 3 integrates the capacity dashboard and smart alerts, followed by end-to-end testing.
So we progress from foundation, to intelligence, and finally to integration.
